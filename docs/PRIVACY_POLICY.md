# Privacy Policy — Torren Relay

**Last updated: 2 September 2026**

Torren Relay is a Chrome extension for freight dispatchers working the
Amazon Relay load board. This policy explains what the extension collects,
why, and where it is stored.

## What we collect

**Email address.** Collected when you create an account and sign in.
This is the only personal information the extension collects.

**Authentication tokens.** Issued by our authentication provider when you
sign in, and stored locally in your browser so you stay signed in between
sessions.

**Your extension settings.** Refresh interval, selected origin cities,
alert preferences and similar options. Stored locally in your browser
using Chrome's storage API.

## What we do not collect

- We do not collect your name, address, phone number or payment details.
- We do not collect, store or transmit your Amazon Relay account
  credentials. The extension never sees them — it works alongside the
  session you are already signed in to in your own browser.
- We do not send the details of the loads you view or book to our
  servers. The extension reads load information from the Amazon Relay
  page in order to display it to you, and that information stays in your
  browser.
- We do not track your browsing outside Amazon Relay domains.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services.

## Where your data is stored

**With our authentication provider.** Your email address and account
record are stored with Supabase, which we use solely for sign-in. The
only information the extension exchanges with Supabase is what is needed
to sign you in and keep you signed in: your email address, the one-time
code you enter, and your session tokens. No load data, no settings and
no usage information is sent there.

**In your own browser.** Your settings, your session tokens, and — while
you are completing sign-in — the email address you entered are stored
locally using Chrome's storage API. Load information the extension reads
while you work is held only for the life of the browser tab.

## How we use it

Your email is used only to create your account, sign you in, and contact
you about the extension if necessary.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third
parties. We do not share it for advertising purposes.

**One feature opens a third-party site at your request.** The inline load
panel has a button that opens the load's route in Google Maps. If you
press it, the stop names and addresses for that load are included in the
Google Maps web address that opens in a new tab, and are therefore
received by Google under their own privacy policy. This happens only when
you press that button, and nothing is sent to Google otherwise.

## Data retention and deletion

Your account record is kept while your account is active. To have your
account and email address deleted, contact us at the address below and
we will remove them. Settings and session tokens held in your browser are
removed when you uninstall the extension or clear its data, and signing
out removes your session tokens immediately.

## Permissions the extension requests

- **storage** — to save your settings and keep you signed in.
- **clipboardWrite** — to copy a picture of a load card to your clipboard
  when you press the camera button.
- **Access to Amazon Relay domains** — the extension only runs on the
  Amazon Relay load board. It does not run on any other website, and on
  Amazon Relay it does not run outside the load board pages.
- **Access to our authentication provider (supabase.co)** — so the
  extension can sign you in and refresh your session.
